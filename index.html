About

Theory-informed
image reconstruction.

Junior Research Fellow at IISc, working with Prof. Kunal N. Chaudhury.

My work focuses on neural denoisers and learned regularizers for inverse imaging, with particular interest in the stability and convergence of iterative methods. I combine operator-theoretic analysis with practical GPU implementations.

I completed my M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence at IISc and my B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering at Jadavpur University. I am interested in PhD research in inverse problems, computational imaging, and theoretically grounded computer vision.
